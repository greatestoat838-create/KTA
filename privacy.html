Privacy Policy.

How KTA Spices protects commercial account data, procurement orders, and executive contact information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register your kitchen, request a Chef Welcome Box, or submit a wholesale quotation inquiry, we collect information including your name, property/business name, contact phone number, email address, and procurement specifications.

2. Use of Information

We use this information exclusively to process quotation requests, deliver Welcome Boxes, schedule commercial consignments, provide batch analysis certificates, and communicate order tracking updates.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We respect the confidential recipes, proprietary blend ratios, and procurement par quantities of our hotel and restaurant partners. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact or order details to third parties.
