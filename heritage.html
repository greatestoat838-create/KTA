Our Heritage

Rooted in the Highlands.
Refined for the World's Best Tables.

For three decades, KTA has forged direct partnerships with generational growers across Kerala's hill country, Kashmir's saffron valleys, and Rajasthan's cumin fields.

The Beginning

A single conviction: quality cannot be compromised at the source.

KTA was founded in the early 1990s by traders who had spent years watching premium spices lose their identity through layers of middlemen. The original mission was simple but radical for its time — go directly to the hills, build trust with the growers, and bring their best produce unchanged to the kitchens that deserved it.

Our first route was Kerala's Wayanad district — the pepper belt. A single vehicle, a single relationship. Today that network spans 40+ regions across India, with every grower registered, every batch numbered, and every delivery traceable.

What We Stand For

Four pillars. No compromises.

Pillar 01
Origin Purity
Every product is sourced from a single, named region. No blends, no unmarked origins. The label tells the complete story.
Pillar 02
Generational Farmers
We work only with families who have farmed the same land for at least two generations — because heritage soil produces heritage flavour.
Pillar 03
Batch Integrity
Every batch carries a unique lot number. Chefs can trace their peppercorns back to the exact harvest date and farm location.
Pillar 04
Chef-Grade Standards
Our grading protocol is set by working executive chefs, not market-standard inspectors. If a chef tastes the difference, so does our QC.
The KTA Journey

Three decades of sourcing discipline.

01
Founded in Wayanad
A single vehicle, a single pepper farm in Wayanad district. The principle of direct-only trade was established from day one — no auction floors, ever.
02
Direct Trade Mandate
Expansion into cardamom, saffron, and cumin — all under formal Direct Trade Agreements with registered grower families. The network doubles.
03
250+ Elite Kitchens
By the 2010s, KTA had become the quiet backbone of hotel kitchens across South India's five-star circuit. Anonymity by design — quality by conviction.
04
Hotel Smart 24/7
Launched India's first origin-certified spice logistics system built specifically for commercial hotel kitchens — guaranteed 24-hour dispatch.
